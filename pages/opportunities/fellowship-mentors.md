## Overview
The SCINet Program is calling <!--has an annual call--> for proposals for funding to support postdoctoral fellows to be mentored by ARS scientists. <!--The FY2026 competition is now complete, and instructions specific to the FY27 call for proposals will be posted here in early FY27.-->
The goal of the fellowships program is to develop the next generation of ARS scientists with expertise in conducting and leading individual and collaborative research using computationally intensive approaches. For examples of successful proposal topics, please see the titles of the postdoctoral fellowship proposals funded in [FY2026]({{ site.baseurl }}/opportunities/fellowship-mentors/fy26-awards), [FY2024]({{ site.baseurl }}/opportunities/fellowship-mentors/fy24-awards), [FY2023]({{ site.baseurl }}/opportunities/fellowship-mentors/fy23-awards), and [FY2022]({{ site.baseurl }}/opportunities/fellowship-mentors/fy22-awards) (review of new proposals was paused in FY2025). 
Each fellow should: (1) be involved in individual and collaborative research that includes substantial computational work and that will leverage SCINet's computing infrastructure; (2) have training and leadership opportunities; and (3) contribute to the overall success of SCNet and the fellowships program and help build research computing capacity at ARS. In addition, each fellow will have the opportunity to take advantage of training courses that build computational literacy, such as in data science, machine learning, bioinformatics, and geospatial analyses on SCINet's high-performance computing clusters (Ceres and Atlas).
All fellows will receive a competitive stipend and travel support. Fellowships are funded for two years. **The mentor is expected to provide the fellow's laptop, office space, and laboratory space (if needed).**

All proposals must be submitted using the [online submission form]( https://forms.cloud.microsoft/g/mbVuRqmn97)<!-- online submission form (TBA)-->. The PI's RL or supervisor must approve the proposal prior to submission, and approval will be indicated on the submission form. (If the principal mentor is an RL, their supervisor is still expected to approve the proposal. Any proposal listing the principal mentor as the approver will not be considered for funding.)
All proposals must use [the fellowships proposal template]( https://usdagcc.sharepoint.com/:w:/s/REE-ARS-SCINetOffice/IQCmOo1ZyXj2TLC3yGApDgsXASJc9ljJmMgAzyQvhLJ2FWU?e=ZrwDMi)<!-- the fellowships proposal template (TBA)-->. **Eligibility:** ARS Category 1, 4, or 6 scientists with RL or supervisor approval. Please note that lead mentors on FY2026 awards are not eligible for an FY2027 award as lead mentor.

## Proposal review process
For the first step in the review process, each research office (RO) will review the proposals submitted from within the respective RO. Each RO will decide which of their proposals to advance for full panel review.
After each RO has selected the proposals to advance for panel review, a review panel will be assembled that includes representation from all four ROs and the SCINet Office. Panelists will evaluate the strength of each proposal in four key areas:
1. **The individual research that the fellow will contribute to or lead.** Why is this project important and how does it address a high-priority research topic? Why is additional research capacity essential at this time?
1. **Feasibility of project for a two-year fellowship.** How is the project positioned so the fellow can make an immediate impact and have success? Can the proposed work be completed in two years?
1. **The professional development opportunities available to the fellow.** How will the collaboration opportunities and experiences offered by this project contribute to the fellow’s professional growth? What training opportunities will be available to them?
1. **Contributions to the success of SCINet and the development of research computing capacity at ARS.** How will the fellow’s efforts help grow ARS’s research computing capabilities across the agency’s research portfolio, including the effective use of SCINet resources? For example, will the fellow contribute training materials or help teach workshops about a particular research computing method or topic?

The panel’s funding recommendations will be subject to final review and approval by ARS leadership. The total number of fellowships awarded will depend on the availability of funds.
